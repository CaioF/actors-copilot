01 / COMMERCIAL RATIONALE
Why native Skool billing still matters
Skool native billing can support community acquisition while app subscriptions retain their
existing payment architecture.
Documented advantages
 A native paid Green Room member can contribute to the paid-member review ecosystem; externally paying
free-invited members cannot leave these native paid-member reviews. [S3]
 Growth Boost may surface the community in search, recommendations and Skool-run advertising; Skool
charges a 30% recurring commission on the members it acquires. [S1, S2]
 Skool lists off-platform payments as a Discovery ranking penalty. The impact on a mixed-payment community is
not quantified. [S4]
 Native subscriptions are USD-only. Fixed GBP and EUR offers must therefore continue through Stripe. [S2]
Commercial interpretation
The recommended structure supports a native $9 Skool offer for community acquisition, while preserving an
authoritative, auditable Stripe subscription lifecycle for premium app features. Growth Boost may provide
incremental acquisition; visibility, reviews, conversion and volume remain subject to Skool policies and performance.
Important qualification. Skool does not provide a supported guarantee of Growth Boost attribution or Discovery
treatment after a member transitions from native paid billing to free invited access. We therefore do not assume
continued attribution in the commercial model.

02 / FEASIBILITY
Decision matrix
Production assessment rule: capabilities without explicit official support are classified NOT
SUPPORTED and excluded from the implementation baseline.
Why the limited hybrid is materially different
Native Skool billing is limited to community-only access because external tier-change, cancellation and subscription-
state interfaces are NOT SUPPORTED by Skool's official integration. Premium app entitlements instead use Stripe's
supported subscription events and the existing app backend.
Non-negotiable design principles
 Only one community exists: every eligible customer sees the same Green Room content.
 Maintain Economy and Business app billing in Stripe: Skool does not support the external lifecycle integration
these plans require.
 State clearly that Skool does not support automated cross-platform cancellation or paid-to-free conversion
through its official integration.
 No shared public founding-member or migration coupon; eligibility and use must be constrained.
 Validate and document the Skool-required administrative transition before representing the cross-platform
journey as complete.
Supported* means Stripe-managed app entitlements plus an administrator-led handoff because Skool does not support
subscription-change or paid-to-free conversion interfaces for external systems.

04 / STANDARD JOURNEYS
What customers should experience
The model preserves simple purchase journeys for customers who remain in their original
billing channel.
Website Stripe purchase
After a confirmed Economy or Business checkout, the customer should see a thank-you page, receive payment
confirmation, obtain the proper app features and receive a free Green Room invitation if not already a member. The
existing Stripe webhook/invitation integration is the implementation starting point; its end-to-end reliability still needs
testing.
Skool-native community purchase
Skool collects the $9 USD community subscription and manages Green Room access within Skool. No premium app
entitlement depends on native Skool billing, because Skool does not support the external lifecycle events needed for
app authorization.
Existing subscribers. For an existing Skool-paying member, the required handoff must be completed before treating
a Stripe app purchase as a replacement subscription. Existing free-invited website subscribers must not be offered
an unnecessary second paid Skool membership.

06 / DISCOUNT GOVERNANCE
One-time transition credit vs. lifetime founder entitlement
Founding-member entitlements and short-term transition credits serve separate purposes and
require distinct, controlled Stripe configurations.
Practical Stripe configuration
 Regular migration: a customer-bound, single-redemption, time-limited promotion code with a one-time coupon.
Amount should reflect the approved overlap policy and verified remaining paid period. [S8, S9]
 Founders: verify permanent eligibility against a founder registry; maintain free Skool membership; use an
appropriately scoped recurring Stripe discount where the founder promise applies. [S2, S8]
 Make currency explicit. A USD amount-off coupon is not automatically an equivalent GBP or EUR discount; use
currency-specific values/configuration. [S8]
 Annual Skool migration: do not assume the unused value equals one $9 monthly payment. Check the remaining
prepaid annual period and establish a written credit/refund policy.
 Do not stack migration incentives with founding discounts or promotional trials without an explicit business rule.
Verify how the current 14-day app trial affects the first invoice.
Example, not a default policy. A regular $9 Skool member moving to $19 Economy might receive up to $9 off the
first Stripe invoice if one full month of Skool billing overlap is accepted and verified; subsequent Economy renewals
remain $19. A founder may qualify for $9 off every monthly Economy invoice instead.

09 / RISKS
Risk register and operating safeguards
Stripe provides supported subscription lifecycle events for app billing. Skool does not support
programmatic paid-to-free conversion or external-member offboarding, so those Skool-
specific steps require administrator action.
RISK SEVERITY CONTROL / ACCOUNTABLE OWNER
Skool + Stripe charge overlap HIGH Customer-success verifies cancellation and paid-through date; migration credit
approved before checkout.
Member removed from Skool during
conversion HIGH Admin-managed removal/re-invitation; use existing account; clear status updates to
customer.
Coupon shared or reused HIGH Stripe customer-bound / single-use codes, expiration and redemption audit.
Founder loses lifetime access CRITICAL Permanent founder flag; never remove founder during app offboarding.
Currency or annual-price mismatch HIGH Product-by-product Stripe test before launch; annual “two months free” copy.
Website buyer canceled; Skool stays
active MEDIUM Stripe event → offboarding task → verified admin action, founders excluded.
Discovery/Boost expectations unmet MEDIUM Track Skool-only ROI; no acquisition forecast without real cohort data.
Duplicate identity / account MEDIUM Verification step; do not use email match as proof of account ownership.
Explicit product and support promises
 Present Skool-to-Stripe as a verified transition request: Skool does not support a one-click cross-platform
subscription conversion.
 Show the customer what they will pay now, when their native Skool renewal ends and whether a transition credit
applies.
 Make it clear that free community access continues only while a qualifying Stripe subscription or lifetime founder
entitlement is active.
 Clearly state that automated external member removal and full tier-change synchronization are NOT
SUPPORTED by Skool's official integration.

11 / FINAL DECISION
Management recommendation and open approvals
A defined commercial model that preserves native Skool acquisition and Stripe app billing
within each platform’s documented operational capabilities.
Approve subject to Skool-required administrative procedures
 Skool-native Green Room subscription for direct Skool discovery, USD-only ($9/month; $90/year).
 Continue Stripe billing for all website purchases and all Economy/Business app subscriptions.
 Keep one shared Green Room and invite qualifying Stripe subscribers free of an additional Skool charge.
 Use customer-verified, one-time Stripe credits for normal Skool-to-app migration when there is paid overlap.
 Give founders distinct recurring entitlements and controlled discounts; never disturb lifetime free access.
Do not approve
 Economy/Business subscription tiers paid through Skool and synchronized to the app.
 A promise of automatic Skool-to-Stripe upgrades or cancellations: these workflows are NOT SUPPORTED by
Skool's official integration.
 Unrestricted public coupons, unlimited credits for former Skool members, or discount stacking without policy.
 A production launch with unverified annual prices, mixed currencies or unresolved
member-invitation/offboarding handling.
Four sign-offs requested from Tracey
DECISION NEEDED APPROVAL
Product ownership Skool native = Green Room only; Stripe = all app subscriptions.
Migration policy End-of-term vs immediate credit; annual prepaid-value treatment.
Founder pricing Final GBP/EUR founding equivalents and eligibility list.
Launch operations Skool-required admin conversion and offboarding, with assigned ownership.
BOTTOM LINE: Preserve Skool-native customer acquisition and Stripe-managed app entitlements. The
administrator-led migration is required because Skool does not support the necessary cross-platform
subscription and paid-to-free conversion interfaces.
