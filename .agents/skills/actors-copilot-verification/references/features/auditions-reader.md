# Auditions and Reader Copilot

## Audition Breakdown and Scene Study

**Entry.** Sidebar Quick Actions → **Audition Breakdown** (`/auditions/new/brief`) or **Scene Study** (`/auditions/new/sides`). Scene Study can show a saved-audition picker above the wizard; choosing one remounts the wizard with stored data, and “Start a new scene instead” returns to a blank flow. A load error leaves manual creation available. On the observed production Economy account no picker was visible before a fixture was saved. Source: `components/auditions/scene-study-entry.tsx`, `scene-study-audition-picker.tsx`, `audition-wizard.tsx`.

**Step 1.** Choose Cinematic, Theater or Commercial. Cinematic sides exposes Full Breakdown / Quick / On-The-Run. Enter Project / Production (`#project`) and Role / Character (`#role`); brief also offers optional casting director, deadline and timezone. The sides standalone UI calls the project “Source Material”. Click Next. Navigation itself is not the validation gate.

**Step 2.** “Upload Casting Brief Email” or “Upload Sides” accepts PDF/DOCX through a file input or text through `textarea[placeholder="Paste your details here..."]`. UI says max 20MB; verify actual processing rather than assuming client-side enforcement. Unsupported file selection has a toast; selected file can be removed. Back returns to basics, Next reaches review.

**Step 3.** “Ready to Generate” shows BASICS and CHARACTER BRIEF or SIDES, with project, role and either file name or text word count. `Generate Breakdown` is disabled unless project, role and content exist; `Back to edit` retains entered content. Test missing project, role and content independently; use the disabled state and button title instead of assuming Next prevents advancement. With synthetic inputs, the production Economy session reached review in both modes and Back retained the text. No provider call was involved in those checks.

**Step 4.** Generate invokes synthesis and then `/api/auditions/analyzeBrief` or `/api/auditions/analyzeSides`; wait for “Generating your breakdown...” to resolve to result or error. Result offers Delete (clears result after confirmation), Print, Save Output, and for sides Alternative Take and coach actions. Save disables during work, checks duplicate project/role/type, and may offer **Enrich existing audition** or **Create new anyway**. Confirm save in `/auditions`, reload and compare records. API errors should leave a useful retry path. AI text is nondeterministic.

The enriched fixture remains in the account. Reverse enrichment, picker selection and deletion were not exercised.

**Enrichment.** On an existing audition, Attach Brief/Sides navigates with `enrichAuditionId`; the wizard pre-fills metadata and updates that record. Test brief→sides and sides→brief separately and confirm both analyses survive after reload, including critical brief facts. The Scene Study picker resumes saved work, a different entry from enrichment. ## Audition Tracker and detail

At `/auditions`, search by project/role and use All, Draft, Processing, Completed filters. Empty search/filter results show “No auditions found”. Cards navigate to `/auditions/[id]`; controls open edit, print, attach missing analysis and delete confirmation. Edit changes project, role, category, casting director and status, then Save Changes persists; Cancel closes without saving. Delete permanently deletes the document; restrict it to a specifically identified disposable fixture with appropriate authorization. Print opens detail with `?action=print`. Detail links to Reader and coach and supports attaching missing analyses. List and picker share `users/{userPath}/auditions`; verify reopening, not merely the local optimistic update. Source: `app/(interior)/auditions/page.tsx`, `[id]/page.tsx`, `lib/auditions/`.

## Reader Copilot

At `/reader-copilot`, choose a saved card with sides text or paste dialogue into the textarea (placeholder begins `SARAH`). **Start Interactive Rehearsal** is disabled when text is empty and shows “Parsing Scene...” during `/api/actor-copilot/parse-script`. Reader fetches up to 15 recent auditions and displays those with non-empty `sidesText`; an absent card does not prove no audition exists. Parsing requires Firebase identity. On success “Choose Your Character” appears; choose the actor role and rehearse. Use Partner Voices, AI voice speed, mute/unmute, Restart Scene, Previous Line, Next Line and microphone controls. Completed runs show “Scene Rehearsal Complete!” and recordings. Test manual/hands-free turns, denied microphone, playback and restart in a browser capable of media; jsdom is insufficient. `/reader-copilot/[id]` loads the selected audition and exits back to detail. Check line order and role selection with a short synthetic two-character script; never assert generated audio by exact timing.

Observed on 2026-11-17 in headless Chrome against production with the seeded Economy session: a synthetic ALEX/SAM scene parsed into both character choices; selecting ALEX exposed the rehearsal controls. Audio capture, playback, progression and persistence were not tested.

**Observed cross-feature failure, 2026-11-17:** From the synthetic brief enriched with pasted sides, tracker search → detail → `Rehearse Scene` reached `Rehearsal Unavailable` with “No script sides text found in this audition.” Both analysis sections had remained visible in detail. A read of the locally configured Firebase project found one matching test record with `hasSides`/`hasBrief` and both performance maps, but no nonempty `sidesText`; the deployed Firebase project identity was not independently confirmed. In the mapped source, `newAuditionDoc()` and `buildMergeUpdate()` in `components/auditions/audition-wizard.tsx` do not persist `formData.sidesText`, while `app/(interior)/reader-copilot/[id]/page.tsx` requires that field. This explains the source-level mismatch and is consistent with the observed error, but the deployed commit was not established. Reproduce with a fresh, uniquely named synthetic fixture before asserting broader scope. No application fix was included in this map change.
